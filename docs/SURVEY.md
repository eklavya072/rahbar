# Primary research kit (User insight — 15 pts)

Send tonight; collect until ~11:00 tomorrow. Report numbers **exactly as collected** — never invent responses.

## Google Form: "Do you know your accident cover?" (2 minutes, anonymous)

1. **Do you have a bank account opened under Jan Dhan (PMJDY) or a RuPay debit card?** Yes / No / Not sure
2. **Did you know a RuPay Jan Dhan card carries free accident insurance of up to ₹2 lakh, valid only if the card was used in the 90 days before the accident?** Knew this / Didn't know
3. **Does your passbook show a yearly ₹20 debit labelled "PMSBY"?** Yes / No / Never checked
4. **Did you know every two-wheeler/car insurance policy includes ₹15 lakh personal accident cover for the owner-driver?** Knew this / Didn't know
5. **Did you know the government pays ₹2 lakh to the family if someone dies in a hit-and-run where the vehicle is never found?** Knew this / Didn't know
6. **Has anyone in your family or close circle been in a serious road accident in the last 5 years?** Yes / No
   - If yes: **Which did they claim?** (tick all) Insurance company · MACT/court case · Hit-and-run scheme · PMSBY / PMJJBY · RuPay card cover · Employer/ESIC · Nothing · Don't know
   - If yes: **What got in the way?** (tick all) Didn't know it existed · Missing papers (FIR, post-mortem, policy) · Too many offices · Someone asked for a commission · Took too long · Other
7. **If an app could read your papers and tell you exactly what you're owed — without uploading them anywhere — would you use it?** Yes / Maybe / No
8. (Optional) **Your state / age group.**

Share in: college groups, family WhatsApp groups, local community groups. Target 30–100 responses.

## 2–3 short calls (10 minutes each)
- **Someone whose family went through a crash claim.** When did you first hear about compensation? Who guided you? What documents were hardest to get? Did anyone ask for a percentage?
- **A bank branch employee / insurance agent.** How often do families come to claim PMSBY / RuPay accident cover? What makes claims fail?
- **A lawyer / DLSA paralegal volunteer (if reachable).** What do families miss? What would help you handle more cases?

## How to use the results in the submission
- One slide/section: "We asked N people": % unaware of RuPay cover, % unaware of the ₹15 lakh owner-driver cover, % of crash-affected families that claimed nothing, top barrier.
- 1–2 short anonymised quotes from the calls.
- Link to the form summary (Google Forms → Responses → Summary).
